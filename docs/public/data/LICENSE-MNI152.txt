mni152_t1.nii.gz is the ICBM 2009a Nonlinear Asymmetric MNI152 T1-weighted
template at 1 mm (TemplateFlow: tpl-MNI152NLin2009aAsym_res-1_T1w.nii.gz),
converted from mni_icbm152_t1_tal_nlin_asym_09a.mnc. It is used by the docs
site demos and, via scripts/prepare-test-data.mjs, by the test suites.

Source: https://www.mcgill.ca/bic/icbm152-152-nonlinear-atlases-version-2009
        https://www.templateflow.org/
